Blockgrove

Drag blocks onto an 8×8 board and fill rows or columns to clear them. Every clear sprouts seeds, and the leaves you earn grow a small garden of your own. Play endless Classic, the Daily Grove that everyone shares, or 60 Bloom Quests.

Restore purchases

Open Settings → Restore Purchases, or tap Restore Purchases on the Grove Pass screen. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. Remove Ads and Grove Pass are restored; leaf packs are one-time purchases and are credited once when bought.

Manage or cancel Grove Pass

Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Blockgrove. To avoid being charged after the 3-day free trial of the yearly plan, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. Grove Pass stays active until the end of the paid period.

Add the widgets

Touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Blockgrove and pick Daily Grove (small) or My Grove (medium). The Daily Grove control can be added from Control Center → Add a Control. Widgets update after you play and reset at local midnight.

Ads

Remove Ads turns off ads between games and the Classic banner. Grove Pass turns off all ads. Rewarded ads (revive, swap tray, double leaves) are always optional.
