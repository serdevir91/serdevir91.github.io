Blockgrove

A calm block puzzle for iPhone, set in a hand-carved wooden tray. Drag pebble blocks onto the 8×8 board and fill a row or a column to clear it. Finished games earn leaves, and leaves grow a garden of your own.

  • 8×8 tray
  • Daily Grove
  • 60 Bloom Quests
  • 12-plot garden

Three ways to play

  • Classic: an endless run that saves on every move.
  • Daily Grove: the same starting board and pieces for everyone on the same date. One scored run a day keeps your sprout streak going; practice runs after that are unlimited.
  • Bloom Quests: 60 goal levels on a winding trail. Clear lines, collect flowers or reach a score within a move limit, for up to three stars.

Grow your grove

Spend leaves to plant and grow 12 garden plots: ferns, tulips, sunflowers, lavender, mushroom rings and bonsai pines, each through four stages from seedling to bloom. Every 25 lines you clear earns a free planting.

Widgets and controls

A small Daily Grove widget shows your sprout streak and today's score, a medium My Grove widget shows your garden and leaves, and a Daily Grove control in Control Center opens today's puzzle.

Free, with optional extras

Blockgrove is free and supported by ads. Remove Ads is a one-time purchase. Grove Pass (monthly, or yearly with a 3-day free trial) removes all ads and adds a free revive every run, 100 leaves a day and three extra boards. Leaf packs are also available. No account or sign-in is needed, and the game plays offline.
