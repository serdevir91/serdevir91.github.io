Restore purchases

Open the Settings tab and tap Restore Purchases in the Grove Pass section, or tap the leaf balance at the top of any tab and use Restore Purchases at the bottom of that screen. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. Remove Ads and Grove Pass are restored. Leaf packs are consumable and are credited once, when bought.

Manage or cancel Grove Pass

Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Blockgrove, or tap Manage subscription on the Grove Pass screen while it is active. The yearly plan starts with a 3-day free trial; to avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. After cancelling, Grove Pass stays active until the end of the current period. Boards you unlocked with leaves are yours to keep; Pass-only boards switch back to Moss when Grove Pass ends.

Leaves didn't arrive

Leaf packs are credited as soon as Apple confirms the purchase. A purchase waiting for approval (for example Ask to Buy) is credited once approved. If leaves are still missing, email us with the date of the purchase.

Ads

Remove Ads turns off the ads between games and the Classic banner. Grove Pass turns off all ads. Rewarded ads (revive, swap tray, double leaves) are always optional; revive and tray swaps can also be paid with leaves. iOS asks about tracking once, after your first finished game; you can change it in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Daily Grove

Everyone gets the same Daily Grove on the same date, and a new one starts at local midnight. Only your first run each day is scored and counts for the sprout streak; Practice runs after that don't change today's score.

Add the widgets

Touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Blockgrove and pick Daily Grove (small) or My Grove (medium). To add the Daily Grove control, open Control Center, touch and hold an empty area, tap Add a Control and search for Blockgrove. Widgets update after you play and reset at local midnight.

Sound, haptics and tips

Sound, haptics and Show tips again are in the Settings tab; sound and haptics can also be switched from the pause menu. Sound follows the iPhone's silent switch.
