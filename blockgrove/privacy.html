Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Blockgrove is a block puzzle game. It has no accounts, asks you for no personal information and never asks for your location. It has no servers of its own and no analytics, and it plays fully offline. The free version shows ads served by Unity Ads, which is the only part of the app that collects data, as described below.

What stays on your device

  • Game progress: scores, Daily Grove results and streaks, quest stars, leaves, your garden, unlocked skins, saved runs and settings.
  • It is stored in the app's private storage and shared only with the Blockgrove widgets on the same device. We never receive it.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

The free version shows interstitial ads when you leave a game over or quest result, a banner in Classic mode, and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch to revive, swap the tray or double your leaves. Ads are provided by Unity Technologies. To serve and measure ads, the Unity Ads SDK collects:

  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, language, app version, approximate location derived from the IP address) and diagnostics.
  • Advertising interactions (ads shown, clicked or completed).
  • The Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you decline, Unity Ads shows non-personalized ads and uses no IDFA.

Unity Ads starts only after your first finished game, and iOS asks about tracking just before that, never on first launch. Unity processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can change your tracking choice anytime in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Buying Remove Ads turns off interstitial and banner ads. With an active Grove Pass, Unity Ads is not started at all.

Other network requests

  • Update check to Apple's public App Store lookup service, containing only the app's bundle identifier.
  • Purchases (Remove Ads, leaf packs and the Grove Pass subscription) are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Sharing

Your Daily Grove result leaves your device only when you share it through the share sheet.

Children

Blockgrove is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all progress stored on your device. Purchases stay with your Apple Account and can be restored. To ask Unity about advertising data, see Unity's privacy policy linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
