Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Blockgrove is a block puzzle game for iPhone. It has no accounts, never asks for your name, email or location, and has no servers or analytics of its own. The developer receives none of your game data. The free version shows ads from Unity Ads, which is the only part of the app that collects data, as described below.

What stays on your device

  • Game progress: scores and your top Classic score, Daily Grove results and streaks, quest stars, leaves, your garden, unlocked boards, saved runs and tip progress.
  • Settings and status: appearance, board, sound and haptics, whether you own Remove Ads or Grove Pass (kept so purchases work offline), the time of the last ad and of the last update check.
  • This is stored in the app's own storage on your iPhone and shared only with the Blockgrove widgets on the same device.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

The free version shows an interstitial ad when you leave a game-over, Daily Grove or quest result screen (at most one every two minutes), a banner at the bottom of Classic games, and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch to revive, swap the tray or double your leaves. Ads are provided by Unity Technologies. To serve and measure ads, the Unity Ads SDK collects:

  • Device and app information (such as device model, iOS version, language, app version and an approximate location derived from the IP address) and diagnostics.
  • Advertising interactions (ads loaded, shown, clicked or completed).
  • The Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you decline, ads are not personalized with the IDFA.

When it starts: Unity Ads does not start when you first open the app. It starts when you leave the result of your first finished game; iOS asks about tracking just before that, once. On later launches it starts when the app opens. Unity processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can change your choice anytime in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or through Open privacy settings under Ads privacy in the app's Settings tab.

Purchases and ads: Remove Ads turns off interstitial and banner ads; optional rewarded ads stay available, so Unity Ads still runs. With an active Grove Pass, Unity Ads is not started at all.

Other network requests

  • Update check: at launch (at most once a day) and when you tap Check for Updates, the app asks Apple's public App Store lookup service whether a newer version exists. The request contains the app's bundle identifier and your device's region code.
  • Purchases (Remove Ads, leaf packs and Grove Pass) are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • Ratings use Apple's own rating prompt.

Sharing

Your Daily Grove result (date, score, lines, streak and an App Store link) leaves your device only when you share it yourself through the share sheet.

Children

Blockgrove is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all progress stored on your device. Remove Ads and Grove Pass stay with your Apple Account and can be restored; leaf balances cannot be restored after deletion. For advertising data held by Unity, see Unity's privacy policy linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
