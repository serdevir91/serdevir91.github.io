Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

Wherelens is a daily geography guessing game. It has no accounts, asks you for no personal information and never asks for your location. Your scores stay on your iPhone. The free version shows ads served by Unity Ads, which is the only part of the app that collects data, as described below.

What stays on your device

  • Game progress: daily scores, streaks, practice counts, country stamps and settings.
  • It is stored in the app's private storage and shared only with the Wherelens widgets on the same device. We never receive it.

Street scenes (Apple Maps)

Each round loads a street-level scene from Apple Maps Look Around for a location chosen by the game. The request contains the round's coordinates, not yours. Apple handles it under the Apple Privacy Policy. Wherelens does not store the imagery.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

The free version shows interstitial ads after a finished daily or between practice rounds, and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for extra practice rounds, hints or region passes. Ads are provided by Unity Technologies. To serve and measure ads, the Unity Ads SDK collects:

  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, language, app version, approximate location derived from the IP address).
  • Advertising interactions (ads shown, clicked or completed).
  • The Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you decline, Unity Ads shows non-personalized ads and uses no IDFA.

Unity processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can change your tracking choice anytime in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Buying Remove Ads or Wherelens Explorer turns off interstitial ads.

Other network requests

  • Update check to Apple's public App Store lookup service, containing only the app's bundle identifier and your region.
  • Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Sharing

Your daily result leaves your device only when you share it through the share sheet.

Children

Wherelens is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all progress stored on your device. To ask Unity about advertising data, see Unity's privacy policy linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
