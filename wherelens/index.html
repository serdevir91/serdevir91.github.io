Restore purchases

Open Settings → Restore Purchases, or tap Restore Purchases on the Explorer screen. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Cancel Wherelens Explorer

Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Wherelens. Explorer stays active until the end of the paid period.

A round says "Finding a street…"

Street scenes load from Apple Maps and need an internet connection. If a spot has no scene, the game picks a nearby replacement automatically.

Scoring

Each round is worth up to 5,000 points; the closer your pin, the higher the score. A daily has 5 rounds, so the best total is 25,000. Everyone gets the same five places on the same day.

Ads

Remove Ads or Explorer turns off ads between rounds. Rewarded ads are always optional.
