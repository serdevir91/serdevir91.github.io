Tillwise · Receipt Scanner

Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

Tillwise is built so your receipts stay on your iPhone. We do not run servers that receive your data, we do not have accounts, and we do not use analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

What stays on your device

  • Receipt images you scan with the camera or import from Photos or Files.
  • Text and details read from them (merchant, date, totals, tax, category), which are extracted on the device using Apple's Vision framework and, where available, Apple's on-device language model.
  • Projects, notes and settings you create.

This data is stored in the app's private storage on your device. It is included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) under Apple's terms. We never receive it.

Network requests

Tillwise makes only these requests, none of which contain your receipts or personal information:

  • Exchange rates from api.frankfurter.app (a free public service using European Central Bank data), when you save a receipt in a foreign currency with Pro. The request contains only the currency codes and date.
  • Update check to Apple's public App Store lookup service, which contains only the app's bundle identifier and your region.
  • Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not see your payment details.

Sharing

Reports and receipts leave your device only when you choose to share or export them, for example by sending a PDF or CSV through the share sheet. Where they go is your choice.

Camera and Photos

Camera access is used only to scan receipts. Imported photos are read only when you pick them; Tillwise has no access to the rest of your library.

Children

Tillwise is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Use Ledger → Delete all data in the app, or delete the app. Both remove everything Tillwise stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
