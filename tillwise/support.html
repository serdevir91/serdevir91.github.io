Restore purchases

Open Ledger → Restore Purchases, or tap Restore Purchases on the Pro screen. Use the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.

Cancel a subscription

Open the iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tillwise, or Ledger → Manage subscription in the app.

A receipt was read wrong

Every field can be edited before saving. Fields Tillwise is unsure about are outlined with "Check this". Flat, well-lit photos read best.

Free plan

The free plan includes 5 scans a month and CSV export. The count resets on the first day of each month.
