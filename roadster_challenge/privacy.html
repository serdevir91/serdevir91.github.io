Official Privacy Policy

Roadster Challenge

Google Play ID: com.serdevir.roadsterchallenge • Standalone: com.erdevirlabs.roadsterchallenge • Publisher: Soner Erdevir (Erdevir Labs)

Offline-First & Privacy Commitment: Roadster Challenge is designed with an offline-first, privacy-respecting architecture. We do NOT collect, harvest, monitor, or sell your Personally Identifiable Information (PII). Your gameplay records, unlocked roadsters, garage tuning upgrades, and coin balances reside safely on your device.

1. Information Collection & Storage

Local Save Storage: Game progress, high scores, lane distance metrics, unlocked vehicles, paint configurations, and custom settings are stored locally on your device within encrypted sandbox storage. We do not operate remote tracking servers or require an online profile to play.

Device Information: The game queries standard platform technical parameters (display resolution, refresh rate, haptic motor capability, audio state, and input controllers) solely to render graphics and provide smooth gameplay at high frame rates. This technical information never leaves your device.

2. In-App Purchases & Monetization

Roadster Challenge offers optional digital purchases to enhance your experience:

  • Ad-Free Edition (remove_ads): A permanent non-consumable license that completely disables third-party advertising across the game.
  • Pro Tier Pass (pro_tier): Unlocks exclusive cyber roadster chassis, experimental tracks, and enhanced nitro recharge rates.
  • All-Access Lifetime Pass (lifetime_unlock): Lifetime entitlement granting all current and future premium features.
  • Supercharger Energy Cell Packs (supercharger_pack): Consumable in-game energy boosts for instant nitro refills.
  • Virtual Coin Packs (coin_pack_small, coin_pack_medium, coin_pack_mega): Virtual currency packs used for garage tuning and aesthetic enhancements.

Secure Store Billing: All financial transactions are processed directly and securely by Google Play Billing, Apple App Store (StoreKit), or the Microsoft Store. Erdevir Labs never collects, processes, or stores your credit card, banking, or billing details. You can restore non-consumable purchases at any time via the "Restore Purchases" button in Settings.

3. Advertising & Ad-Free Option

For players using the standard version without the Ad-Free upgrade, the game may display non-intrusive banner or interstitial advertisements served through authorized ad networks (such as Google AdMob, Unity LevelPlay, or store partners). These SDKs may collect anonymized, non-identifying telemetry (such as device type and advertising ID) in accordance with Google Play and Apple developer policies.

Permanent Ad Removal: Purchasing the Ad-Free Edition or Pro Tier permanently suppresses all advertisement SDK requests across the entire application.

4. Diagnostics & Crash Reporting

The application may utilize standard anonymous crash telemetry provided by Google Play Console, Apple App Store Connect, or Microsoft Dev Center. These diagnostics are purely technical (such as GPU backtraces and operating system build versions) and are used exclusively to resolve bugs, optimize physics calculations, and maintain high FPS performance.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Roadster Challenge is an arcade racing experience intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If you believe personal information has inadvertently been provided, please contact us for immediate deletion.

6. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Know / Access: All user game data is contained in your device's local application storage.
  • Right to Deletion: You can completely erase your data at any time via the in-game "Reset Progression" option in Settings, or by clearing application data / uninstalling the app.
  • No Sale of Data: We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer personal information to third parties.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect platform compliance updates or new features. Any modifications will be posted to this URL with an updated effective date.
