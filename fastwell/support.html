Start, edit or end a fast

In the Fast room, tap the plan chip inside the ring to pick a plan, then tap Start fast. If you began fasting earlier, tap Started earlier? to choose a start time up to 72 hours ago. While a fast is running, tap Started … · Edit under the ring to adjust the start time, or Change plan to switch your goal. Tap End fast when you are done to log your mood and an optional note.

Connect or manage Apple Health

Open Settings → Apple Health (or tap Connect Apple Health in the Body room) to allow Fastwell to save and read Dietary Water and Body Mass. To change permissions later, open the iPhone Health app, tap your profile picture in the top right, tap Apps, and select Fastwell. If Apple Health is off, Fastwell still saves every glass of water and weight entry locally on your iPhone.

Live Activity and widgets

When you start a fast with Live Activity enabled in Settings, Fastwell puts a live countdown on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. iOS displays a single Live Activity for up to 8 hours; opening Fastwell during a longer fast refreshes it automatically. To add Home Screen or Lock Screen widgets, touch and hold the screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and choose Fastwell.

Restore purchases

Open Settings (top-left button) and tap Restore Purchases under Fastwell Plus, or tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the Fastwell Plus paywall. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account used for the original purchase. Both subscriptions and the Lifetime unlock are restored.

Manage or cancel Fastwell Plus

While a subscription is active, tap Manage Subscription in Settings → Fastwell Plus, or open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name (Apple Account), and tap Subscriptions. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. To avoid being charged after the yearly plan's 1-week free trial, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Fastwell Plus Lifetime is a one-time purchase and never renews. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Export or delete your data

With Fastwell Plus, open Settings → Data → Export CSV to export your fasts, water entries and weight entries as standard CSV files. To erase all local data stored in Fastwell, tap Settings → Data → Delete all data.

General wellness notice

Fastwell is a general wellness timer and journal, not a medical device or medical advice. Talk to a qualified healthcare professional before fasting if you are pregnant, under 18, have diabetes, take medication or have a history of eating disorders.
