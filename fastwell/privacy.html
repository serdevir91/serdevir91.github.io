Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Fastwell is an intermittent fasting timer and journal for iPhone, made by Soner Erdevir. Fastwell has no accounts, no servers of its own, no advertising SDKs, no tracking and no analytics. We do not collect, store or share your personal data.

What stays on your device

  • Your fasting journal: active and past fast start and end times, selected plans, mood ratings and notes.
  • Body logs: water and weight entries you log in Fastwell.
  • Settings: appearance mode, ring theme, unit preferences, water goal, notification preferences, onboarding state, last update check timestamp, review prompt state, and your Fastwell Plus entitlement mirror.

All of this data is stored locally on your iPhone inside the App Group container shared only between the Fastwell app and its widgets on the same device. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

Connecting Apple Health is completely optional. Fastwell asks for permission only after an in-app explanation, and only for two data types: Dietary Water (dietaryWater) and Body Mass (bodyMass).

  • Saving: when allowed, water and weight entries you log in Fastwell are saved to Apple Health on your iPhone.
  • Reading: when allowed, Fastwell reads your water samples for today and recent weight samples from Apple Health so your daily water ring and weight chart stay complete.
  • On-device only: Apple Health data read by Fastwell never leaves your iPhone. It is never sent to any server, never used for advertising, marketing or analytics, and never shared with third parties. CSV exports include only entries you logged directly in Fastwell.

You can turn Apple Health access on or off at any time in the iPhone Health app under your profile → Apps → Fastwell. Fastwell works in full even when Apple Health access is not granted.

No advertising or tracking

Fastwell contains no ads, no third-party analytics SDKs, and never requests App Tracking Transparency permission.

Network requests made by the app

  • Update check: at most once every 24 hours at launch, and when you tap Check for Updates in Settings, Fastwell queries Apple's public iTunes Lookup endpoint with the app's bundle identifier and region code to check if a newer version is available on the App Store.
  • In-app purchases: Fastwell Plus Weekly, Fastwell Plus Yearly and Fastwell Plus Lifetime are processed directly by Apple through StoreKit 2. We never receive your billing or payment details.
  • Ratings: rating prompts use Apple's standard StoreKit review dialog.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual fasts, water entries or weight entries inside the app, or remove everything at once in Settings → Data → Delete all data. Deleting the app from your iPhone also removes all local Fastwell data and settings. Samples already saved to Apple Health remain in the Health app under your control until you delete them there.

Children

Fastwell is a general-audience wellness tool and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
