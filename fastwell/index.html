Fastwell

A quiet intermittent fasting timer, gentle stages, water and weight tracking, and a private journal on your iPhone.

A calm timer and eating window

  • Ember ring: pick a plan, tap Start fast, and watch the circular ring fill toward your goal with elapsed time, remaining time and finish time.
  • Gentle stages: Digesting, Settling, Deep fast, Extended and Long fast share general wellness context on what people often notice along the way.
  • Flexible timing: backdate a start time up to 72 hours, adjust your plan mid-fast, or follow your eating window countdown after a fast ends.

Plans for every rhythm

Start free with 13:11 Gentle and 16:8 Classic. Unlock 18:6 Focused, 20:4 Narrow window, OMAD 23:1, 36h Day and a half, and custom plans from 12 to 72 hours with Fastwell Plus.

Water, weight and Apple Health

Log glasses of water in metric (ml) or imperial (fl oz) and track your weight trend over 30 days, 90 days or 1 year. Optionally connect Apple Health so water and weight you log in Fastwell are saved to Health, and water and weight from other apps appear in your Fastwell charts. Everything works in full even if you do not connect Apple Health.

Live Activity, widgets and Siri

Follow your fast from the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island with a Live Activity, add Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, toggle your fast from Control Center, or ask Siri how long you have fasted.

Free and Fastwell Plus

Free: 13:11 and 16:8 plans, the timer and stages, eating window, Live Activity, all widgets, Control Center control, Siri Shortcuts, notifications, daily water logging (8-glass goal), Apple Health water saving, and the last 14 days of history and stats.

Fastwell Plus: every plan plus custom 12–72h lengths, unlimited history and full stats with personal insights, weight tracking and charts, custom water goals, 4 extra ring themes, and CSV export. Available as a weekly ($2.99) or yearly ($29.99, with a 1-week free trial for eligible users) auto-renewable subscription, or a one-time Lifetime purchase ($49.99). US prices; your local price is shown in the app.

Private by design

No account, no sign-in, no ads and no tracking. Your journal and Apple Health data stay on your iPhone and are never sent to any server. Fastwell provides general wellness information only and is not medical advice.
