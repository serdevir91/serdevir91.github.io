Homespun

A cozy little studio for your iPhone Home Screen and Lock Screen. Design a widget in the app, place it from the widget gallery, and choose your design with Edit Widget.

Seven kinds of widgets

  • Photo: a favorite picture with a caption, in small, medium or large.
  • Countdown: days, and hours if you like, to any date.
  • Calendar: a full month or a week strip; you choose the first day of the week.
  • Quote: over 180 classic public-domain quotes, a new one each day.
  • Clock: analog or digital, with an optional date.
  • Checklist: up to five items you can tick right on the widget, with an optional reset every morning.
  • Moon: today's phase, illumination and the next full moon, calculated on your iPhone.

Made in a real editor

A live preview at true widget size, solid, gradient, photo or pattern backgrounds, five fonts, text color, corner accents (dot, tape, stitch or sticker) and three layouts: Classic, Bold and Minimal.

12 theme packs

Use a pack to create a matched set in one tap, or restyle every design you already have. Linen, Matcha Latte, Coastal and Mono Minimal are free; the other eight come with Homespun Plus. You can also try one Plus pack free for 24 hours by watching a short, optional ad.

Free and Plus

Free: up to 3 designs, 4 packs, a one-photo Photo widget, and the Countdown and Moon circles for the Lock Screen.

Homespun Plus: unlimited designs, all 12 packs, photo widgets that rotate up to 10 photos every hour, every Lock Screen style (adds Countdown and Quote rectangles and a Checklist count) and your own quotes. Weekly ($2.99) or yearly ($19.99, with a 3-day free trial for eligible users) subscription, or Lifetime ($34.99, one time). US prices; your local price is shown in the app.

Private by design

No account, no sign-in, no analytics. Photos are chosen with the system photo picker and resized copies stay on your iPhone. Widgets never show ads. Homespun makes widgets; it does not change other apps' icons.
