Add a widget to your Home Screen

First save a design in Homespun. Then touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Homespun and pick the widget type and size. Touch and hold the placed widget, tap Edit Widget and choose the design to show. If none is chosen, it shows your newest design of that type.

Add a Lock Screen widget

Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize → Lock Screen, tap the widget area and pick a Homespun widget. Countdown and Moon circles are free; the other Lock Screen styles need Homespun Plus.

Restore purchases

Open Settings → Restore Purchases in Homespun, or tap Restore Purchases on the Homespun Plus screen. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. Both the subscription and the lifetime unlock are restored.

Manage or cancel Homespun Plus

Tap Manage Subscription in Homespun Settings, or open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Homespun. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period; cancel the yearly plan at least 24 hours before its 3-day free trial ends to avoid a charge. Plus stays active until the end of the paid period. The lifetime unlock is a one-time purchase and never renews.
