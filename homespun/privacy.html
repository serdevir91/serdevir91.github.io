Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Homespun is a widget studio for the iPhone Home Screen and Lock Screen, made by Soner Erdevir. It has no accounts and no servers of its own, uses no analytics, and never asks for your location, contacts or calendar. The only part of the app that collects data is the optional rewarded ad served by Unity Ads, described below.

What stays on your device

  • Your designs: widget styles, names, countdown dates, checklist items and your own quotes.
  • Settings: appearance, first day of the week, haptics, whether onboarding is done, the last update check, the first launch date and a count of saved designs (both used only to decide when to ask for a rating), your Homespun Plus status and any active pack trial.
  • Photos: resized copies of the photos you pick (see below).

All of this is kept in storage shared only between the Homespun app and its widgets on the same iPhone. It is never sent to us.

Photos

Photos are chosen with the system photo picker, so Homespun has no access to your photo library and never asks for library permission. It only receives the photos you pick. For each one it saves a resized JPEG copy (at most 800 pixels on the long side) for the widget to display. The copy does not keep the original's location or camera metadata. Photos are never uploaded or shared. Deleting a design also deletes photo copies that no other design uses.

Calendar, clock, quotes and moon

The calendar widget draws a calendar grid from your iPhone's date settings; it does not read your calendar events. Clock, quote and moon phase widgets are calculated on the device from built-in data. Widgets make no network requests.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

Homespun shows rewarded ads only: no banner or interstitial ads, and never ads in widgets. On a locked theme pack you can choose to watch an ad to try that pack for 24 hours. Unity Ads is not started at launch or during onboarding. It starts the first time you open a locked pack, either in the Packs tab or from a locked pack style in the editor, and iOS asks about tracking once, just before that. Unity Ads is never started while Homespun Plus is active. Ads are provided by Unity Technologies. To serve and measure ads, the Unity Ads SDK collects:

  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, language, app version, approximate location derived from the IP address) and diagnostics.
  • Advertising interactions (ads shown, clicked or completed).
  • The Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you decline, ads are not personalized with the IDFA.

Unity processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can change your tracking choice anytime in the iPhone Settings app under Privacy & Security → Tracking, or from Ads privacy → Open Settings in Homespun.

Other network requests

  • Update check: at most once a day at launch, and when you tap Check for Updates, Homespun asks Apple's public App Store lookup service for the latest version. The request contains only the app's bundle identifier and your region code.
  • Purchases: Homespun Plus Weekly, Homespun Plus Yearly and Homespun Plus Lifetime are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • Ratings: rating prompts use Apple's standard review dialog.

Children

Homespun is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting a design removes it and its unused photo copies. Deleting the app removes all designs, photo copies and settings from your iPhone. Purchases stay with your Apple Account and can be restored. To ask Unity about advertising data, see Unity's privacy policy linked above.

Changes
