Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Homespun is a widget studio for your Home Screen and Lock Screen. It has no accounts, asks you for no personal information and never asks for your location. It has no servers of its own and no analytics. The only part of the app that collects data is the optional rewarded ad served by Unity Ads, as described below.

What stays on your device

  • Your designs: widget styles, countdown dates, checklist items, your own quotes and settings.
  • Your photos: see below.
  • All of it is stored in the app's private storage and shared only with the Homespun widgets on the same device. We never receive it.

Photos

Photos are chosen with the system photo picker, so Homespun never gets access to your photo library and never asks for library permission. It only receives the photos you pick. For each one it saves a resized copy (at most 800 pixels) on your iPhone for the widget to display. The copy does not keep the original's location or camera metadata. Photos are never uploaded or shared. Deleting a design also deletes photo copies that no other design uses.

Calendar, clock and moon

The calendar widget draws a calendar grid from your iPhone's date settings; it does not read your calendar events. Clock and moon phase widgets are calculated on the device. Widgets make no network requests.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

Homespun shows no banner or interstitial ads, and never shows ads in widgets. On a locked theme pack you can choose to watch a rewarded ad to try that pack for 24 hours. Unity Ads is started only the first time you open a locked pack's details, and iOS asks about tracking just before that, never at launch. It is not started while Homespun Plus is active. Ads are provided by Unity Technologies. To serve and measure ads, the Unity Ads SDK collects:

  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, language, app version, approximate location derived from the IP address) and diagnostics.
  • Advertising interactions (ads shown, clicked or completed).
  • The Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you decline, Unity Ads shows non-personalized ads and uses no IDFA.

Unity processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can change your tracking choice anytime in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Other network requests

  • Update check to Apple's public App Store lookup service, containing only the app's bundle identifier.
  • Purchases (the Homespun Plus subscription and the lifetime unlock) are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

Homespun is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all designs and photo copies stored on your device. Purchases stay with your Apple Account and can be restored. To ask Unity about advertising data, see Unity's privacy policy linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
