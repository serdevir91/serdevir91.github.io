Privacy Policy for Markdown Keeper

Application ID: www.markdownkeeper.app • Effective Date: October 7, 2026

100% Local-First & Personal Cloud Storage
Executive Summary: Markdown Keeper is built with privacy by design. Your notes, checklists, images, audio recordings, and concept map diagrams are stored locally on your device. When you enable Google Drive sync, your data synchronizes directly with your own personal Google Drive account. We do not operate external database servers that harvest or store your private notes.

1. Information We Collect and Process

A. Note Content and Media Attachments

All note text (Markdown), checklist items, tags, folder organization, image attachments, audio voice memos, and canvas concept map connections are stored locally in the application's private SQLite database on your device.

B. Google Drive Synchronization

If you choose to sign in with Google to enable automatic cloud backup and cross-device sync:

  • Authentication is handled directly through Google Sign-In and OAuth 2.0.
  • Markdown Keeper requests limited scope access (drive.file) to create and maintain a dedicated folder named "Markdown Keeper Notes" inside your personal Google Drive.
  • The application only accesses and manages files that it creates within that folder. It does not inspect, read, or modify any other files in your Google Drive.

C. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Markdown Keeper offers an optional Pro subscription (Monthly and Yearly). All billing transactions, renewals, and payment data are processed exclusively by Google Play In-App Billing. We never collect or store your payment cards or financial account numbers.

D. Advertisements (Free Tier)

The free version of Markdown Keeper may display banner advertisements provided by certified ad networks (such as Unity Ads and Google AdMob). These networks may collect pseudonymous device identifiers and usage statistics in accordance with their privacy policies to deliver compliant advertising. Upgrading to Markdown Keeper Pro removes all advertisements completely.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide local note editing, checklist tracking, and visual concept map connections.
  • To backup and synchronize your notes across your devices using your own Google Drive storage.
  • To process and unlock Pro features (such as Concept Map and Ad-Free mode) via Google Play Billing.
  • To import and export Markdown (.md) documents and ZIP archives upon your explicit request.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal notes or private information to third parties. Third-party SDKs integrated into the app include:

  • Google Play Services: Core authentication and in-app purchase verification.
  • Google Drive API: Direct cloud storage integration with your own Google Drive.
  • Unity Ads / Google AdMob: Ad serving for non-pro users.

4. Account and Data Deletion (Markdown Keeper)

Developer: Erdevir Labs • Application: Markdown Keeper (www.markdownkeeper.app)

In accordance with Google Play User Data and Account Deletion policies, Markdown Keeper provides users with complete transparency and autonomous controls to delete their accounts, cloud linkages, and all associated personal data at any time.

How to Delete Your Account Connection & Associated Data:

  • In-App Account Disconnection: Open Markdown Keeper → Go to Settings → Under Google Drive Cloud Sync, tap "Sign Out". This immediately purges all OAuth session tokens and severs any link between the app and your Google account.
  • Deleting Local Notes & Files: You can delete any note, checklist, or media attachment directly in the app. Empty the Trash to permanently delete them. Uninstalling the application or clearing the app storage in your Android device settings instantly deletes 100% of locally stored data and cached databases.
  • Deleting Cloud Backups on Google Drive: Open your Google Drive (app or web) → Locate the "Markdown Keeper Notes" folder → Permanently delete the folder. You may also visit Google Account Permissions and revoke access for Markdown Keeper at any time.
  • Direct Deletion Request by Email: Users can request complete assistance or manual data verification by emailing our developer team at serde.dev91@gmail.com with the subject line "Markdown Keeper - Data Deletion Request". Requests are verified and processed within 48 hours.

Types of Data Deleted vs. Retained:

  • Data Deleted: All Markdown text, checklists, audio memos, photo attachments, tags, folder structures, OAuth tokens, and Google Drive synchronized files upon user deletion.
  • Data Retained: None. Erdevir Labs does not maintain proprietary third-party servers collecting your private notes or identity records. Subscription financial billing records are processed and retained exclusively by Google Play In-App Billing per Google's statutory compliance obligations.

5. Security

We implement standard best practices to safeguard your data. Communication with Google Drive occurs over secure HTTPS/TLS encryption. All local data is isolated within the application's sandboxed storage.

6. Children's Privacy

Markdown Keeper is a general-audience productivity tool and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
