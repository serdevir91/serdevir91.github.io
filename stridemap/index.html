Stridemap

Stridemap turns your everyday walks into virtual journeys along famous real-world routes for iPhone. Pick a route, go about your normal day, and watch your marker move along an Apple Maps route from town to landmark.

12 iconic world routes

  • Free routes: Camino Francés (Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port to Santiago de Compostela, 780 km) and Amalfi Coast (Sorrento to Salerno, 65 km).
  • Stridemap Explorer routes: Route 66, The Tokaido, Inca Trail, Great Wall Ramble, West Highland Way, Cappadocia Loop, Iceland Ring Road, Pacific Coast Highway, Danube Trail and Cape Town to Cape Point.

Illustrated postcards and certificates

Every landmark you reach unlocks an illustrated procedural postcard for your Postcard Album with route notes and reach dates, plus live Apple Maps Look Around imagery at landmarks where available. Completing a route awards a framed Journey Certificate you can customize and share.

Your daily walking journal

The Today drawer sits right over your map with a daily step ring, distance walked, route ribbon progress, a 7-day chart and your daily goal streak. Walk without your iPhone? Use Add a walk to log steps or distance by hand for today or the past six days.

Walk Mode, Live Activities and widgets

Tap Start walk for live step counting during a walk and a Lock Screen and Dynamic Island Live Activity showing elapsed time, steps and distance to the next landmark. Place Today Steps and Journey widgets on your Home Screen and Lock Screen, plus a Start Walk button in Control Center.

Free and Stridemap Explorer

Free: Camino Francés and Amalfi Coast (one active journey at a time), the full Today panel, Walk Mode with Live Activities, all widgets and Control Center controls, manual walks and the Trailhead certificate style. You can also watch an optional rewarded video ad to unlock a 24-hour journey pass or preview the next landmark postcard.

Stridemap Explorer: all 12 journeys, running several journeys at the same time, custom walking reminders, three additional certificate styles (Atlas, Midnight, Botanical), three map themes (Muted Atlas, Terrain 3D, Night Route) and no ad offers. Monthly ($3.99) or yearly ($19.99, with a 3-day free trial for eligible users) auto-renewable subscription, or Lifetime ($39.99, one time). US prices; your local price is shown in the app.

Private by design

No account, no sign-in and no location tracking. Stridemap reads step count and walking/running distance from Apple Health on your iPhone and never writes to Apple Health or uploads your data. Stridemap is for motivation and fun, not medical advice.
