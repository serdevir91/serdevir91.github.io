Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Stridemap: Walking Journeys is made by Soner Erdevir. Stridemap has no user accounts, no servers of its own, no first-party analytics, and never asks for your GPS location, contacts or photos. All Apple Health data and journey progress stay on your iPhone. The only third-party SDK that collects data is Unity Ads when you use optional rewarded ads on the free plan, described below.

Apple Health and Motion data (on-device only)

  • Read-only Apple Health access: If you choose to connect Apple Health, Stridemap requests read access to two quantity types only: step count (HKQuantityTypeIdentifierStepCount) and walking/running distance (HKQuantityTypeIdentifierDistanceWalkingRunning). Stridemap never writes any data to Apple Health.
  • Motion & Fitness: When you tap Start walk in Walk Mode, Stridemap uses CMPedometer to count live steps during that walk.
  • Never shared or uploaded: Your step counts, distances and motion data are processed exclusively on your iPhone to advance your virtual route marker, update your daily step ring and refresh your widgets. Health and motion data are never sent to any server, never logged externally, and never shared with Unity Ads or any third party.
  • Works without Apple Health: Connecting Apple Health is optional. If you decline or revoke access, you can log steps or distances manually with Add a walk.

What stays on your device

  • Your walking journal: active, paused and completed journey runs, unlocked postcards, certificate style and optional name on certificates, manual walk entries, cached 30-day step totals, and walking reminder times.
  • Settings: appearance mode, distance units, daily step goal, map style and theme, haptic preference, and Stridemap Explorer entitlement status.

This data is stored locally in an App Group container shared only between the Stridemap app and its widget extension on the same iPhone.

Maps and Look Around

Stridemap displays routes and optional landmark Look Around imagery using Apple Maps (MapKit). Stridemap never requests your real-time device location; the "you are here" marker represents your virtual distance along a route. Look Around scenes are fetched live from Apple Maps when you open a landmark sheet and are never cached or stored.

Advertising (Unity Ads — Rewarded Only, No Tracking)

Stridemap shows optional rewarded video ads only on the free plan (no banner ads, no interstitial ads, and never ads in Walk Mode, widgets or Live Activities). You can choose to watch a rewarded ad to try a locked journey for 24 hours or preview the next landmark postcard. Unity Ads is not initialized at launch or during onboarding; it initializes only after onboarding when you open the Journeys picker or a Landmark sheet while on the free plan. Unity Ads is never initialized while Stridemap Explorer is active.

Stridemap does not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission and does not access the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA). To serve and measure contextual rewarded ads, the Unity Ads SDK may collect:

  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, language, app version, IP-derived coarse location, and vendor/device identifiers) and diagnostics.
  • Advertising interaction events (whether a rewarded video was started, completed or clicked).

Apple Health data is never passed to Unity Ads. Unity processes ad data under its Privacy Policy.

Other network requests

  • Apple Maps: map tiles and live Look Around scenes are loaded from Apple's MapKit servers.
  • Update check: at most once every 24 hours at launch, and when you tap Check for Updates in Settings, Stridemap queries Apple's public iTunes Lookup endpoint with the app's bundle identifier to check if a newer version is available.
  • Purchases: Stridemap Explorer subscriptions and Lifetime unlock are processed by Apple via StoreKit 2. We never receive your payment card details.

Wellness disclaimer

Stridemap is for motivation and fun, not medical advice. It does not diagnose, treat or monitor any health condition.

Children

Stridemap is a general-audience application and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual manual walks in the app, revoke Apple Health access anytime in iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Stridemap, or uninstall the app to remove all local journal data and settings from your iPhone.
