How journeys work

Tap the journey chip in the top-left corner of the map to pick a route. Every step recorded after you start or resume a journey moves your marker along the route. On the free plan, one journey runs at a time (switching pauses your current journey and saves your walked distance). With Stridemap Explorer, multiple journeys can run simultaneously.

Connecting or fixing Apple Health steps

Stridemap reads step count and walking/running distance from Apple Health. If Today shows No steps from Apple Health yet, open the iPhone Settings app, go to Health → Data Access & Devices → Stridemap, and make sure Steps and Walking + Running Distance are turned on. Pull down on the Today panel to refresh.

Logging a walk without Apple Health

You can use Stridemap without Apple Health or top up days when you walked without your iPhone. Open the Today drawer, tap Add a walk, pick today or any of the previous six days, and enter steps (100 to 60,000) or distance. Manual walks count toward your daily goal and every active journey. Swipe left on a manual walk entry to remove it from daily totals.

Walk Mode and Live Activities

Tap Start walk in the Today drawer to open Walk Mode and start a Lock Screen and Dynamic Island Live Activity. Walk Mode uses Motion & Fitness step counting for live updates and automatically ends after 4 hours or when you tap End walk.

Adding Home Screen, Lock Screen and Control Center widgets

Touch and hold an empty area on your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and choose Stridemap to add the Today Steps or Journey widget. On iOS 18+, open Control Center, tap the + button, choose Add a Control, and select Start Walk.

24-hour journey pass (rewarded video)

On the free plan, when a rewarded video ad is ready, locked journeys offer Try free for 24 hours. Watching the short video to completion unlocks a 24-hour pass for that route. When the pass expires, your walked distance and postcards stay saved.

Restore purchases

Open Settings (the gear button on the map) and tap Restore Purchases under Explorer, or tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the Stridemap Explorer paywall. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Manage or cancel Stridemap Explorer

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name (Apple Account), then tap Subscriptions and select Stridemap. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period (or the 3-day free trial on the yearly plan). Explorer Lifetime is a one-time purchase and never renews. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
