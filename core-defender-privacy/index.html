Roguelite Hybrid Strategy

Core Defender: Rogue Survivor

Explore the ruins. Discover lost arts. Fortify your tower. Survive the swarm.

A thrilling fusion of fast-paced survivor bullet-heaven action and tactical tower defense. Scout mysterious ancient battlegrounds, link power generators, unearth forgotten relics, and construct impenetrable bastions around your arcane Core Crystal against colossal horde invasions.

Key Gameplay Features

Survivor & Tower Defense

Defend yourself and your stationary power core simultaneously. Balance aggressive exploration with defensive fortification.

Weapon Evolutions & Fusions

Master arcane weapons to rank 5 and forge legendary fused armaments with dual devastation capabilities.

Tactical Fortifications

Construct ballistas, frost emitters, mortar artillery, tesla arcs, and fortified gates from field salvage.

Colossal Siege Guardians

Conquer multi-phase boss titans and survive massive 420-invader siege swarms across 3 distinct proving grounds.
