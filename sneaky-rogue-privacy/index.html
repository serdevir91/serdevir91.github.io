Sneaky Rogue - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 29, 2026 | Last Updated: September 29, 2026

Application ID: com.sneakyrogue.app Developer: Erdevir Labs

This Privacy Policy explains how Sneaky Rogue ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects player data. We are committed to ensuring that your privacy is protected while you enjoy our 2D Roguelike Snake arcade game.

Privacy Policy (English)

1. Data We Process

Sneaky Rogue is designed with a privacy-first mindset. The game processes only minimal operational and gameplay information:

  • In-Game Progression: High scores, maximum dungeon level reached, coins collected, unlocked snake hero characters, and permanent shop upgrade levels.
  • Local Preferences: Audio toggles (sound effects, background music), visual theme settings (Light, Dark, pure black AMOLED mode), and selected language.

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

All gameplay progression, settings, and player stats are stored strictly locally on your device using encrypted local storage. We do not maintain any personal user databases, external profiling servers, or tracking registries.

3. Third-Party Services & Integrations

The game integrates standard, reputable third-party SDKs to provide game services, monetization, and updates:

  • Unity Ads: Displays non-intrusive advertisements (banner and interstitial ads). Unity may process device identifiers and ad interactions in accordance with their privacy standards. You can read their policy at Unity Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Handles optional in-app purchases (such as Remove Ads and gold coin bundles). Google manages all financial and payment card information securely; we never access or store your financial details. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Games Services: Provides optional high-score leaderboards and achievements. Signed-in scores are submitted to Google Play Services according to your Google account preferences.

4. Children's Privacy

Sneaky Rogue is an arcade video game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. All ad requests are configured to respect privacy and family policy guidelines.

5. Your Rights and Data Deletion

Since all gameplay data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your data. You can erase all game history, records, and preferences at any time by:

  • Clearing the app's cache and storage data via your device's Settings > Apps > Sneaky Rogue > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the application from your device.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new game features or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be published directly to this URL with an updated effective date.

Gizlilik Politikası (Türkçe)

1. İşlenen Veriler

Sneaky Rogue gizlilik odaklı olarak tasarlanmıştır. Oyun yalnızca temel oynanış verilerini işler:

  • Oyun İlerlemesi: En yüksek skor, ulaşılan maksimum zindan seviyesi, toplanan altınlar, kilidi açılan yılan karakterleri ve kalıcı mağaza yükseltmeleri.
  • Kullanıcı Tercihleri: Ses efektleri, müzik anahtarları, görsel tema tercihleri (Açık, Koyu, AMOLED saf siyah) ve dil seçimi.

2. Verilerin Saklanması

Tüm oyun ilerlemesi ve tercihler yalnızca cihazınızın yerel hafızasında saklanır. Tarafımızca hiçbir harici sunucuda kullanıcı profili veya kişisel veri kaydı tutulmamaktadır.

3. Üçüncü Taraf Servisler

Oyun içerisinde güvenilir platform servisleri yer almaktadır:

  • Unity Ads: Banner ve geçiş reklamlarını yönetir. Unity Gizlilik Politikası.

  • Google Play Games: İsteğe bağlı küresel lider tabloları ve başarımları sağlar.

4. Çocukların Gizliliği

Oyun genel aile ve her yaştan kitleye uygundur. Çocuklardan herhangi bir kişisel veri toplanmaz ve reklam istekleri içerik kurallarına uygun olarak iletilir.
