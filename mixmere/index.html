Restore purchases

Open Settings → Restore Purchases, or tap Restore Purchases in the Shop. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Cancel Mixmere Studio

Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Mixmere. Studio stays active until the end of the paid period.

Drops

Drops are earned by solving puzzles (up to 3 per level) and can be bought in the Shop. They pay for hints and extra undos. Purchased drops are added to your wallet once and are not restored on a new device.

Ads

Remove Ads or Studio turns off ads between levels. Rewarded ads are always optional.

Color-blind assist

Turn on Settings → Color-blind assist to show pigment letters and mix percentages on every dish.
