Mixmere · Color Pour Puzzle

Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

Mixmere is a color-mixing puzzle game. It has no accounts and asks you for no personal information. Your puzzle progress stays on your iPhone. The free version shows ads served by Unity Ads, which is the only part of the app that collects data, as described below.

What stays on your device

  • Game progress: solved levels, best move counts, drops, daily streak and settings.
  • It is stored in the app's private storage and shared only with the Mixmere widgets on the same device. We never receive it.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

The free version shows interstitial ads between levels and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for hints, undos or extra drops. Ads are provided by Unity Technologies. To serve and measure ads, the Unity Ads SDK collects:

  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, language, app version, approximate location derived from the IP address).
  • Advertising interactions (ads shown, clicked or completed).
  • The Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you decline, Unity Ads shows non-personalized ads and uses no IDFA.

Unity processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can change your tracking choice anytime in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Buying Remove Ads or Mixmere Studio turns off interstitial ads.

Other network requests

  • Update check to Apple's public App Store lookup service, containing only the app's bundle identifier and your region.
  • Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Sharing

A puzzle result card leaves your device only when you share it through the share sheet.

Children

Mixmere is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all progress stored on your device. To ask Unity about advertising data, see Unity's privacy policy linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
