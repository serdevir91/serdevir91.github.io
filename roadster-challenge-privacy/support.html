Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchases on a new device?

Open the in-game Settings menu from the main screen and tap "Restore Purchases". As long as you are logged into the same Google Play or Apple ID account, all non-consumable licenses (such as Ad-Free Edition and Pro Tier) will be immediately restored.

Can I play Roadster Challenge completely offline?

Yes! Roadster Challenge features a 100% offline-ready architecture. Your high scores, unlocks, garage vehicles, and coin balances are saved locally on your device without requiring an active internet connection.

What control schemes are supported?

Roadster Challenge supports intuitive on-screen touch steering, lane swipe gestures, and external gamepad / keyboard controllers on supported devices.

How do I remove ads?

You can remove third-party ads by purchasing either the Ad-Free Edition or the Pro Tier Pass in the in-game store.
