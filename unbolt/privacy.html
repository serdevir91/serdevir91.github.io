Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Unbolt is a single-player screw-removal puzzle for iPhone. It has no accounts, never asks for your name, email or precise location, and has no servers or analytics of its own. The developer receives none of your game data. The free version shows ads from Unity Ads, which is the only part of the app that collects data, as described below.

What stays on your device

  • Game progress: levels won, coins, booster inventory, Workshop restoration steps and dates, Daily Workbench results and streaks, unlocked pegboards, saved boards and coach tip progress.
  • Settings and status: appearance mode, pegboard selection, sound and haptics toggles, whether you own Remove Ads or Workshop Pass (mirrored locally so purchases work offline), the time of the last interstitial ad and the time of the last update check.
  • This data is stored in the app's App Group container on your iPhone and shared only with the Unbolt widgets on the same device.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

The free version shows an interstitial ad when you leave the Win sheet from level 6 onward (at least 90 seconds apart), a 320×50 banner at the bottom of the Level screen, and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for boosters, a revive or double coins. Ads are provided by Unity Technologies. To serve and measure ads, the Unity Ads SDK collects:

  • Device and app information (such as device model, iOS version, language, app version and coarse location derived from the IP address) and diagnostics.
  • Advertising interactions (ads loaded, shown, clicked or completed).
  • The Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you decline, ads are not personalized with the IDFA.

When it starts: Unity Ads does not start when you first open the app. It initializes after you win level 3; iOS asks about tracking just before that first initialization, once. On subsequent launches after that first start, Unity Ads initializes when the app opens. Unity processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can change your choice anytime in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or through Change in iOS Settings under Ads & Privacy in the app's Settings screen.

Purchases and ads: Remove Ads turns off interstitial and banner ads; optional rewarded ads stay available, so Unity Ads still runs. With an active Workshop Pass, Unity Ads is not initialized at all.

Other network requests

  • Update check: at launch (at most once every 24 hours) and when you tap Check for Updates in Settings, the app queries Apple's public App Store lookup service to check whether a newer version is available. The request sends only the bundle identifier and your device's region code.
  • Purchases (Remove Ads, coin packs and Workshop Pass) are processed by Apple through StoreKit 2. We never see your payment details.
  • Ratings use Apple's standard in-app rating prompt.

Sharing

Your Daily Workbench summary (date, moves, streak and an App Store link) leaves your device only when you choose to share it through the iOS share sheet.

Children

Unbolt is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all progress stored on your device. Remove Ads and Workshop Pass remain linked to your Apple Account and can be restored; consumable coin balances cannot be restored after deletion. For advertising data held by Unity, see Unity's privacy policy linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
