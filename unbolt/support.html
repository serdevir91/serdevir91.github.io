Restore purchases

Tap the wrench button at the top left of the Workbench to open Settings and tap Restore Purchases in the Workshop Pass section, or tap the coin pill at the top right and use Restore Purchases at the bottom of the paywall. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Remove Ads and Workshop Pass are restorable; consumable coin packs are credited once when bought.

Manage or cancel Workshop Pass

Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Unbolt, or tap Manage subscription on the Workshop Pass screen while your pass is active. The yearly plan starts with a 3-day free trial; to avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. After cancelling, Workshop Pass stays active through the end of the paid period. Pegboards unlocked with coins stay yours permanently; Pass-included boards revert to Garage if the subscription expires.

Coins didn't arrive

Coin packs (800 Coins and 5,000 Coins) are credited immediately once Apple confirms the transaction. Purchases pending approval (such as Ask to Buy) unlock automatically as soon as they are approved. If coins are still missing, email us with the approximate time of purchase.

Ads and tracking

Remove Ads turns off interstitial ads and the Level screen banner while keeping optional rewarded ads available. Workshop Pass disables all ads completely. iOS asks for tracking permission once, right after you win level 3; you can update your choice anytime in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking or via Settings → Ads & Privacy in Unbolt.

Daily Workbench

The Daily Workbench unlocks after level 4. Every player gets the same board for a given calendar date, resetting at local midnight. Your first win each day punches your time card, advances your streak and awards streak coins.

Add the widgets and control

Touch and hold an empty area on the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Unbolt and choose Daily Workbench (small or Lock Screen) or Workshop (medium). To add the Control Center shortcut, open Control Center, touch and hold an empty spot, tap Add a Control and search for Daily Workbench.

Sound, haptics and tips

Sound, haptics and Show tips again live in Settings (the wrench button on the Workbench); sound and haptics can also be toggled from the in-level pause sheet. Workshop sounds respect the iPhone's silent switch.
