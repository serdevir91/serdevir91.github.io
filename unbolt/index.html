Unbolt

A tactile screw-removal puzzle for iPhone, set on a restorer's workshop wall. Unscrew overlapping wood, brass, brushed steel and enamel plates from an 8×10 pegboard, route matching drive heads into tool crates, and put every win toward restoring twenty vintage antiques.

How it plays

  • Unscrew & sort: tap any uncovered screw to back it out. Matching open crates at the top take three screws of the same colour and drive glyph; everything else waits on your 5-slot wooden bench tray.
  • Drop the plates: when a plate loses its last screw, it pivots and drops away, uncovering the plates and screws pinned beneath it.
  • X-ray inspection: press and hold the board to turn overlapping plates translucent and plan around hidden screws.
  • Colourblind-safe: every screw colour carries its own engraved drive glyph (slotted, cross, hex, star, square, triangle, tri-wing and spanner).

Restore twenty antiques

Every first-time win on the Level trail cleans or polishes one part of the antique on your workbench—from a c. 1905 Pocket Watch and a Typewriter to a Gramophone, Brass Telescope, Model Locomotive and Music Box. Completed finds move to your seven-shelf Gallery.

Daily Workbench & widgets

After level 4, a fresh Daily Workbench board unlocks every day at local midnight—the same board for every player. Keep your 7-day punch card and streak alive, and track your progress from Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets or the Control Center control.
