Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Wordloom is a crossword word puzzle game for iPhone. It has no accounts, never asks for your name, email or precise location, and has no servers or analytics of its own. The developer receives none of your game data. The free version shows ads from Unity Ads, which is the only part of the app that collects data, as described below.

What stays on your device

  • Game progress: solved levels, in-progress level and Daily Weave states, woven tapestry bands, coins, bonus jar count, lifetime words and bonus words, daily results and streaks, unlocked loom themes, and tip progress.
  • Settings and status: appearance mode, selected loom theme, sound, haptics and Tap to spell toggles, whether you own Remove Ads or Loom Club (mirrored locally so purchases work offline), processed transaction IDs, and the timestamps of the last interstitial ad and update check.
  • This data is stored in the app's local App Group container on your iPhone and shared only with the Wordloom widgets on the same device.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

The free version uses the Unity Ads SDK to show a banner at the bottom of the puzzle screen, an interstitial ad when leaving a solved main level's complete screen from level 5 onward (at least 90 seconds apart), and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for Spotlight hints, a free Reveal Letter when coins are short, or doubling a level's coins. Ads are provided by Unity Technologies. To serve and measure ads, the Unity Ads SDK collects:

  • Device and app information (such as device model, iOS version, language, app version and coarse location derived from IP address) and diagnostics.
  • Advertising interactions (ads loaded, shown, clicked or completed).
  • The Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you decline, ads are not personalized with the IDFA.

When it starts: Unity Ads does not start when you first open the app. It initializes for the first time when you leave the complete screen of level 3 (or later); iOS asks for tracking permission just before that first initialization. On subsequent launches after that, Unity Ads initializes when the app opens. Unity processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can change your tracking choice anytime in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or via Change in iOS Settings under Ads privacy in Wordloom's Settings.

Purchases and ads: Buying Remove Ads or subscribing to Loom Club disables all ads (banner, interstitial and rewarded) and prevents Unity Ads from initializing.

Other network requests

  • Update check: at launch (at most once every 24 hours) and when you tap Check for Updates in Settings, the app queries Apple's public iTunes lookup service using the app's bundle identifier and your device's region code.
  • Purchases (Remove Ads, coin packs and Loom Club) are processed by Apple through StoreKit 2. We never see your payment details.
  • Ratings use Apple's built-in StoreKit review prompt.

Sharing

Your Daily Weave summary text or rendered tapestry image leaves your device only when you choose to share it yourself through the iOS share sheet.

Children

Wordloom is a general-audience family-friendly word game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all progress stored on your device. Remove Ads and Loom Club remain linked to your Apple Account and can be restored with Restore Purchases; consumable coin balances cannot be restored after deletion. For advertising data held by Unity, see Unity's privacy policy linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
