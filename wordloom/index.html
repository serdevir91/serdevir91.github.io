Wordloom

A crossword-style word game for iPhone set on a weaver's table. Swipe across the letters on a round spindle to spell words, watch each word shuttle into the grid as woven weft, and weave geometric city tapestries one band at a time.

  • 180 levels
  • 12 city tapestries
  • Daily Weave
  • Word of the Day

How it plays

  • Swipe the spindle: trace across 5 to 7 letters on the wheel (or turn on Tap to spell in Settings) to form words of 3 or more letters.
  • Weave the grid: every crossword word fills its cells with a plain-weave check. Extra valid words fly into your bonus jar for extra coins (+1 coin each, +25 coins for every 10 bonus words).
  • Use hints anytime: spend coins on Reveal Letter, Reveal Word or Spotlight, or shuffle the wheel whenever you want a fresh angle.

Twelve cities, one thread

Journey through 12 chapters of 15 levels each: Kyoto, Marrakech, Lisbon, Oaxaca, Istanbul, Reykjavik, Cusco, Hanoi, Florence, Cape Town, Quebec City and Jaipur. Each solved level weaves one 3-row band of that city's tapestry from the bottom up. Finishing all 15 bands hangs the completed textile in your Tapestry Gallery with two short facts about the city and a shareable card.

Daily Weave & widgets

Play the same 6-letter Daily Weave as everyone else on the same calendar date to build your streak. Add the small Daily Weave widget (and Lock Screen widgets) to track your streak, the medium Word of the Day widget to learn a fresh word every morning, and the Daily Weave control in Control Center.

Free, with optional extras

Wordloom is free and supported by ads. A one-time Remove Ads purchase turns off all ads permanently. Loom Club (monthly, or yearly with a 3-day free trial) removes all ads and adds 3 free Reveal Letter hints every day, the Saffron, Midnight Silk and Rose Wool loom themes, and early access to the next city from band 8. Coin packs are also available. No account or sign-in is required, and the game plays offline.
