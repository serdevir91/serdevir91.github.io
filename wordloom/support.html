Restore purchases

Open Settings from the bottom spool rail on Home and tap Restore Purchases under Purchases and app, or open the paywall (by tapping the coin pill at the top right) and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account used for the original purchase. Remove Ads and Loom Club are restored automatically; consumable coin packs are credited once at the time of purchase.

Manage or cancel Loom Club

Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Wordloom, or tap Manage subscription on the Loom Club card in the paywall while your membership is active. The yearly plan includes a 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers; cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends to avoid being charged. After cancelling, Loom Club stays active until the end of the current billing period. Loom themes bought with coins remain yours permanently; Club-only themes revert to Linen when Loom Club expires.

Coins didn't arrive

Coin packs (600 Coins and 4,000 Coins) are credited as soon as StoreKit confirms the transaction. If a purchase is pending approval (such as Family Sharing Ask to Buy), coins are added automatically once approved. If coins are still missing, email us with the date of purchase.

Ads and tracking

Both Remove Ads and Loom Club turn off all banner, interstitial and rewarded ads. When ads are off, Spotlight and other hints use coin prices. In the free version, iOS asks about tracking once right before ads start (after your third finished level); you can change your choice anytime in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking or from Settings → Ads privacy in the app.

Daily Weave and streaks

Everyone gets the same Daily Weave puzzle on the same calendar date, resetting at local midnight. Solving consecutive calendar days grows your daily streak; missing a full calendar day resets the streak.

Add the widgets and Control Center control

Touch and hold an empty area on your iPhone Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Wordloom, and choose Daily Weave (small) or Word of the Day (medium). You can also add Daily Weave to your Lock Screen or add the Daily Weave button in Control Center via Add a Control.

Tap to spell, sound and haptics

Prefer tapping letters instead of swiping? Turn on Tap to spell in Settings → Play (it turns on automatically when VoiceOver is running). Sound, haptics and Show tips again are in the same section.
