User Data Deletion Instructions

Core Defender Data Deletion

Compliance with Google Play & Apple App Store user data deletion guidelines.

Account-Free, Client-Side Architecture: Core Defender: Rogue Survivor does NOT require user account creation, social sign-in, or remote database synchronization. All save states, gold currency, and upgrades exist exclusively in local device storage.

How to Delete Your Game Data Instantly

You can erase all your personal progression data at any moment through two simple methods:

  1. In-Game Reset:
    • Launch Core Defender: Rogue Survivor.
    • Select the SETTINGS tab on the main menu.
    • Scroll to the Progression section and click "RESET PROGRESSION DATA".
    • All local statistics, banked gold, records, and hero unlock data will be wiped permanently from your device.
  2. System Storage Clearance / App Uninstall:
    • Android: Open device Settings → Apps → Core Defender → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage.
    • Windows: Open Settings → Apps → Installed Apps → Core Defender → Advanced Options → Reset or uninstall the application.
    • iOS: Delete the app from your home screen. Sandboxed local data is automatically purged by iOS upon app removal.

Remote In-App Purchase Records

Financial records of your purchases (such as the Ad-Free Edition) are maintained by Google Play, Apple App Store, or Microsoft Store under their platform terms. Erdevir Labs holds no server copies of transaction records. If you wish to request transaction deletion from the platform store, please consult your store account settings directly.
